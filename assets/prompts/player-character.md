# Player Character Asset Prompt

## Top-Down Player Sprite

Create a top-down player character sprite for a futuristic roguelike shooter.

Subject:
Armored human pilot / sci-fi survivor, readable as a human from a top-down camera.

Style:
Dark sci-fi, neon cyberpunk, Hades meets Vampire Survivors, clean professional indie game asset, sharp armor shapes, glowing cyan visor and small blue energy accents.

Perspective:
True top-down or slightly angled top-down game sprite, facing right/east as the default aiming direction.

Pose:
Combat-ready stance holding a compact futuristic pistol or energy rifle pointed to the right.

Silhouette:
Clear human torso, helmet, shoulders, arms, legs, and weapon. The character must stay readable at small size.

Color palette:
Dark navy and black armor, cyan/blue highlights, tiny white specular accents. Avoid red because enemies use red.

Resolution:
1024x1024 master, 1:1 square. Final in-game export can be downscaled to 256x256.

Background:
Transparent PNG.

Animation planning:
Design should support later 8-direction aiming and simple walk-cycle variants.

Naming convention:
`player_survivor_topdown_base.png`

No text.
No watermark.
No environment.
No shadow baked into the sprite.

## Optional Sprite Sheet Follow-Up

Create a top-down player character sprite sheet for a futuristic roguelike shooter.

Subject:

Frames:
8 directional idle/aim frames: east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest, north, northeast.

Layout:
4 columns by 2 rows.

Resolution:
2048x1024 sprite sheet, each frame 512x512.

Background:
Transparent PNG.

Consistency:
Same armor, helmet, weapon, cyan visor, proportions, and lighting across every frame.

No text.
No watermark.
